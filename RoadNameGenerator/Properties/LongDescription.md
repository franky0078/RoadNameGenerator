Road Name Generator automatically assigns road names based on the selected naming style.


**Supported naming styles**

- American road names
- British road names
- German road names
- Italian road names
- French road names
- Swedish road names
- Spanish road names
- Australian road names
- Canadian road names
- Polish road names
- Portuguese road names (Portugal)
- Dutch road names
- Swiss road names (german, french, italian, romansh)
- Norwegian road names
- Danish road names
- Mexican road names


**Features**

- Automatically names newly created roads
- Can rename existing roads
- Several naming styles and road categories
- Avoids duplicate road names
- Custom road names: Create your own name list
- Flexible name sources: Use a predefined naming style, your custom list, or a mix of both
- Customize any naming style by excluding unwanted road names.
- Quick style selection: Switch between all 19 naming styles and your custom list directly ingame
- Lightweight and runs automatically in the background
- Language support for English, German, French, Italian, Spanish and Polish
- Optional logging for troubleshooting

The naming style and other options can be configured in the game settings.


**Important notice**

Road names are only generated or updated while the game simulation is running and game time is advancing.

If the game is paused, newly created or changed roads will be processed after the simulation is resumed.

Road Builder roads are detected and classified very reliably in most cases, based on their Road Builder category and road layout.
